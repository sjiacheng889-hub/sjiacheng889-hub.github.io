Frequently Asked Questions

Background reading: how load changes liters per hour and choosing 50Hz vs 60Hz for your grid.

How much fuel does a 200 kW generator use?▼

At 100% load the 200 kW set consumes about 54 L/h (41.6 L/h at 75%, 29.2 L/h at 50%). Actual consumption depends on load profile, ambient conditions and fuel quality.

Is the 200 kW set prime or standby rated?▼

It is rated at 200 kW prime (250 kVA) and 220 kW standby (275 kVA). Prime rating suits continuous or variable load; standby rating is for limited emergency hours. Confirm the duty with your engineer before ordering.

What voltage and frequency can it run at?▼

400/230V 50Hz & 380/220V 60Hz, selectable 50 or 60 Hz. The alternator is calibrated per order; confirm your local grid configuration.

Can this generator be supplied for Vietnam?▼

This model is actively supplied to Vietnam, Philippines, Thailand, Malaysia, Myanmar. Import duties and documentation depend on the destination country, HS code and applicable trade agreements (for example ACFTA Form E eligibility). Confirm requirements before shipment.

What options are available (open, silent, container)?▼

Open skid, silent canopy, containerized and trailer configurations are available on request. Options affect noise level, dimensions, weight and price.
