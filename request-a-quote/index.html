How your RFQ is handled

STEP 01

You send the details

One form covers all five product lines. WhatsApp and email both reach the same export desk at the Luchuan factory in Guangxi.

STEP 02

An engineer checks the specification

We verify load, frequency, ambient temperature and altitude against the Yuchai rating, and tell you plainly if the set needs derating or a heavier tropical radiator.

STEP 03

Quotation within 24–48 hours

Itemised pricing, dispatch time, freight option and the datasheet for the exact model you asked for. No obligation to buy.

STEP 04

Production, test and shipping

Every genset runs a full-load and 110% overload load-bank test before packing, and the test record travels with the unit.

Questions buyers ask before sending an RFQ

How quickly will I get a reply to my RFQ?▼

An engineer answers within one working day and a full itemised quotation follows within 24 to 48 hours. Spare-part and service-kit enquiries are usually answered the same day, because in-stock items are dispatched in one to two days.

What do you need in order to quote a diesel generator set?▼

Country, load in kW or kVA, prime or standby duty, frequency and installation type. If you already know the model, pick it in the generator-set tab; if you only know the load, send that and we will recommend the set.

Can I request a quote for spare parts or a service kit only?▼

Yes. Choose Spare Parts or Service Kits, select the part number or the service interval, and add your engine series so we confirm fitment before quoting. There is no minimum order for stock parts.

Do you quote FOB or CIF?▼

Both. We quote ex-works Luchuan, FOB Nansha or Qinzhou, and CIF to Haiphong, Manila, Klang or Bangkok. Add your destination port in the form and freight is listed separately from the unit price.

Which documents come with the shipment?▼

Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, the factory load-bank test record and an ISO 9001 copy. ACFTA Form E is issued where the HS code, origin rules and destination tariff schedule allow preferential duty.
