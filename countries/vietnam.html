Frequently Asked Questions

What voltage and frequency is used in Vietnam?▼

220V / 380V · 50Hz. Confirm the site requirement (voltage, frequency and power factor) before ordering so the alternator is calibrated correctly.

Do I need a certificate to import a generator into Vietnam?▼

CR Mark – only mandatory for products within QCVN/CR scope (8502 gensets: confirm coverage by HS before shipment). Requirements depend on the product (HS code), rating, importer registration and current regulations — verify with the authority or your customs broker before shipment.

Can I use ACFTA Form E for import to Vietnam?▼

Preferential tariff treatment may be available under ACFTA subject to the applicable HS code, Vietnam tariff schedule, origin rules and customs procedures. Confirm eligibility with your customs broker.
