YC4A140L-D20 Yuchai Diesel Engine

YC4A Series Series · YC4A系列

The YC4A140L-D20 is a 4-cylinder Inline-4 diesel engine from Yuchai's YC4A Series, rated at 95 kW prime power for generator-drive applications. With 4.21L displacement and turbocharged aspiration, it delivers reliable power for diesel gensets in the 95–105 kW range.
