Diesel Generators for Islands & Marine Harbors

Salt air kills ordinary sets fast. For island power and harbor installations we use an anti-salt-spray epoxy paint system, stainless exterior hardware, and a tropicalized heavy-copper radiator sized for ambient up to 50 degrees C, because an island afternoon in the tropics will find any weak point in the cooling system.

Above a few hundred kilowatts the practical answer is a containerised set: a CSC-certified 20 ft or 40 ft housing that ships as one unit, lifts straight onto the barge, and keeps the salt and the weather off the engine and the switchgear. Tell us the landing conditions and the load profile and we will recommend open, silent or containerised.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you protect the set from salt corrosion?▼

Island and harbor units get an anti-salt-spray epoxy paint system, stainless-steel exterior hardware, and protection on the exhaust and cooling circuits. Combined with a tropical radiator rated to about 50 degrees C ambient, this is what keeps a coastal set running reliably between services.

When should I choose a containerised set?▼

For installations roughly 500 kW and above, or any site exposed to sea wind and rain, a containerised set is usually the cleanest answer. The CSC-certified 20 ft or 40 ft housing ships as one lift-ready unit and shelters the engine, the alternator and the switchgear from the weather.

Can one set carry a whole island or harbor load?▼

It depends on the peak and the diversity of the load. For a growing island supply we often recommend two or more sets in parallel so that maintenance does not mean a blackout. Send the load profile and we will propose a rating and a paralleling arrangement that matches the demand curve.
